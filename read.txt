This is a sales-performance finding, not profitability, since cost data is unavailable.Location Tier: Tier 3 outlets generated the highest total sales at ₹76,36,752.63, followed by Tier 2 and Tier 1.Outlet Type: Supermarket Type1 generated the highest total sales at ₹1,29,17,342.26, while Supermarket Type3 had the highest average sales per record at ₹3,694.04.Pricing: The ₹100–200 MRP range generated the highest total sales at ₹1,08,05,874.74. However, the ₹200+ range had the highest average sales per record at ₹3,678.82.Top Outlet: OUT027 (Supermarket Type3, Tier 3) was the highest-performing individual outlet, generating ₹34,53,926.05 with an average sales value of ₹3,694.04.Visibility: High visibility did not necessarily correspond to higher sales. The High Visibility group had the lowest average sales (₹1,825.08), while the Low Visibility group had the highest (₹2,299.54). This shows an observed relationship, not causation.Category–Outlet Strategy: Fruits and Vegetables were the top category for Supermarket Type1 and Type3, while Snack Foods were the top category for Supermarket Type2 and Grocery Stores.
